What is a bonsai?

If we translate literally from Japanese, the word bonsai means “tree that grows on a tray”. But a tree does not grow by itself in a pot. The different care it receives: watering, frequent fertilization, repotting and prunning, among others, allow the bonsai “to fully express the greatness of trees that live in the wild”.
